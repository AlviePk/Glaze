    Slips aren’t glazes

    The color guess, surface map and crazing check are built for glazes, so they don’t apply to a slip. What matters for a slip is that it shrinks along with your clay.

    • Brush or dip it on leather-hard or bone-dry clay, as its recipe says.
    • If it cracks or flakes as it dries, it’s shrinking more than the clay. Apply it thinner, or add a little more of a non-shrinking material like calcined kaolin or silica.
    • Set the recipe back to a glaze above if this one is really a glaze.
